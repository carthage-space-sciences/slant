SLANT
Solar Limb Absorption of Neutrals in the Thermosphere

Carthage College's entry in the NASA TechLeap Orbital Clarity Challenge: a sub-kilogram solar-occultation sensor that measures thermospheric mass density and the composition of atomic oxygen, molecular nitrogen and molecular oxygen from a host satellite.

Evidence site in preparation

This address will hold the figures, analyses, drawings, board design and test records behind the submission, with the date and basis of each. It is published, and frozen, when the submission is made.
